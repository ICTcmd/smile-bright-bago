Our Story

What is Smile Bright Bago?

Smile Bright Bago is an official program of the Local Government Unit of Bago City, Negros Occidental. Established in 2022, the program was created in response to the growing need for accessible dental health services among residents of Bago City, particularly among children, senior citizens, and low-income communities.

The program provides free dental check-ups, tooth extraction, cleaning, and oral health education to all residents, including mobile dental clinics, community outreach, and preventive care services — all at no cost to qualified beneficiaries.

Through partnerships with local health centers, barangay officials, and volunteer dental professionals, Smile Bright Bago has reached over 5,000 beneficiaries across all 48 barangays of Bago City.

Our Foundation

Mission, Vision & Values

🎯

Mission

To provide accessible, compassionate, and quality dental health care to all residents of Bago City — especially children, senior citizens, and the underprivileged — through free dental services, oral health education, and community outreach programs that promote healthy smiles and overall well-being.

🌟

Vision

A Bago City where every resident has access to quality dental care, where no one suffers from preventable dental problems, and where every child and adult enjoys a healthy, confident smile through the support of their local government.

💎

Core Values

Compassion — We serve with genuine care and empathy.

Integrity — We act with honesty, transparency, and accountability.

Excellence — We strive for the best dental health outcomes.

Community — We believe in the power of collective action.

🏛️

LGU Commitment

The Local Government Unit of Bago City fully supports Smile Bright Bago as a flagship dental health program, allocating dedicated budget, dental professionals, and mobile dental clinics. The program operates under the City Health Office with direct oversight from the Office of the Mayor.

Government Support

LGU Involvement

Smile Bright Bago operates under the direct supervision of the Bago City Local Government Unit, with full institutional support from the City Mayor's Office and the City Health Office (CHO).

The program is funded through the city's annual health budget, supplemented by grants from the Department of Health (DOH) and partnerships with private dental clinics and organizations committed to oral health promotion.

Barangay officials and Rural Health Unit staff serve as the program's frontline implementers, ensuring dental services reach every resident in need across all 48 barangays.
